Why Your Health Plan Costs What It Costs

Social Media Video Series Checklist — 33 videos across 5 clusters

Prepared for Will Brown — The Benefits Group

0 of 33 posted

Cluster A — Foundational Mindset & Vocabulary

5 videos

Everything downstream depends on these five videos. No prior knowledge assumed.

Video 1. Wait — You're Not Just Insured, You're Buying Something

Reframes the entire series: presenting your insurance card isn't a free pass, it's a purchase. Every claim spends real money that comes back around in premiums.

Video 2. The $16,000 Toilet Seat Problem

Explains moral hazard using the classic government-waste analogy: when nobody feels the bill directly, nobody has a reason to say no.

Video 3. Fully Insured vs. Self-Funded vs. Level-Funded: What Plan Do You Actually Have?

Plain-language definitions of the three funding models, since every later video assumes the viewer knows the difference.

Video 4. Meet the Five Players Running Your Health Plan

Quick-hit overview of the network, PBM, TPA, underwriter, and stop-loss carrier — the cast of characters every plan has, regardless of size.

Video 5. It's Not Arbitrary: Why Your Premium Is What It Is

Kills the idea that premiums are a random number handed down from the carrier. Premium = expected claims + admin fees + risk charge.

Cluster B — How Pricing Actually Gets Built

6 videos

Unpacks each of the five players from Cluster A one at a time, plus how claims turn into next year's number.

Video 6. What Does "In-Network" Really Mean? (Hint: Not "Cheap")

Dispels the assumption that in-network automatically means the best price. Networks are built on discounts off inflated billed charges, not a true market price.

Video 7. The Most Controversial Player in Your Plan: The PBM

Explains what a pharmacy benefit manager actually does, and why PBMs are facing lawsuits and new transparency laws.

Video 8. Who's the TPA, and Why Do They Process Your Claims?

Defines the third-party administrator role — the entity actually adjudicating and paying claims on a self-funded or level-funded plan.

Video 9. Stop-Loss Insurance: Your Employer's Safety Net (and Why It Matters to You)

Explains stop-loss coverage in plain terms — insurance that protects the employer (and indirectly, the plan) from catastrophic claims.

Video 10. How Underwriters Decide What You'll Pay

Introduces claims-experience-based pricing: underwriters set the maximum expected claim liability based on the group's health status and history.

Video 11. The Renewal Loop: How This Year's Claims Become Next Year's Bill

Closes Cluster B by connecting every individual claim back to the premium conversation from Video 5 — creating the "every claim has a consequence" payoff.

Cluster C — Where the Oversight Breaks Down

7 videos

Requires the vocabulary from Clusters A and B. This is the "fraud, waste, and abuse" heart of the series.

Video 12. Who's Actually Auditing Your Claims? (Spoiler: Almost No One)

Lays out the oversight gap directly: carriers may only closely audit claims above a high dollar threshold, and smaller plans get even less scrutiny.

Video 13. Why Small Businesses Get Less Protection Than Big Ones

Explains the size gap in oversight: large self-funded employers often pay for independent claim auditors; small and level-funded plans usually don't.

Video 14. When Your Employer Becomes Legally Liable: The Fiduciary Duty You Didn't Know Existed

Introduces ERISA fiduciary responsibility using the Johnson & Johnson PBM lawsuit as the real-world hook.

Video 15. The Document Your Employer Is Supposed to Give You (and Rarely Does)

Explains the Summary Plan Description (SPD) — what it is, why a benefits booklet from the carrier isn't the same thing, and the real penalty for non-compliance.

Video 16. Same Drug, Same Patient, Wildly Different Price

Uses a real multi-hospital price comparison for the same cancer drug (Avastin) to show price variation with zero quality difference.

Video 17. Why a $200 Conversation Loses to a $200,000 Surgery

Names the perverse incentive structure head-on: providers are paid far more to treat than to prevent.

Video 18. Follow the Money: Where Did Your Premium Dollar Actually Go?

Uses insurer revenue growth data to show the scale of consolidation and where premium dollars have been flowing over the last decade.

Cluster D — Your Playbook: Shopping Like It's Your Money

10 videos

The behavior-change core of the series. Requires Clusters A and C — viewers need to understand the incentive problem before the fix makes sense.

Video 19. Before You Book Anything: Check the Provider's Track Record

Teaches members to check quality ratings before agreeing to a procedure, instead of assuming a referral is automatically the right (or only) choice.

Video 20. The Question That Could Save You Thousands: "Is There an Outpatient Option?"

A single question members can ask before surgery: whether an outpatient or ambulatory setting is available instead of inpatient.

Video 21. Shopping for Imaging Like You Shop for Anything Else

Applies consumer-shopping behavior to imaging (MRI, CT, X-ray) instead of defaulting to wherever the referring provider sends you.

Video 22. Standalone Surgery Centers & the "Single Case Agreement" Move

Introduces ambulatory surgery centers and single case agreements (SCAs) as a lower-cost, provider-negotiated alternative to hospital-based procedures.

Video 23. Five Ways to Pay Less for the Same Prescription

A tight, practical list: generics over brand, 90-day fills, manufacturer copay cards, and Patient/Manufacturer Assistance Programs (PAPs/MAPs).

Video 24. When an International or Specialty Pharmacy Makes Sense

Covers international and specialty pharmacy options for expensive, ongoing medications, with a clear caution that this requires planning ahead.

Video 25. Direct Primary Care: Cutting Out the Middleman for Everyday Care

Explains the direct primary care (DPC) model — a flat-fee relationship with a primary care provider outside the traditional insurance billing cycle.

Video 26. Read Your EOB Like a Detective

Teaches members to actually read their Explanation of Benefits and catch billing errors or duplicate charges instead of filing it unread.

Video 27. Virtual Care First, ER Last

Positions telehealth and virtual behavioral health as the default first stop for non-emergency issues, not a last resort.

Video 28. Use a Free Concierge to Shop For You

Introduces care navigation and advocacy services that many plans already include at no extra cost, which will shop, negotiate, and handle prior authorization on the member's behalf.

Cluster E — Toolkit & Capstone

5 videos

Wraps the series: account types, plan design levers, and the closing call to action.

Video 29. HSA vs. HRA vs. FSA: Which Account Actually Fits How You Spend?

Side-by-side comparison of the three account types — who owns it, who funds it, and whether unused funds roll over.

Video 30. High Deductible Doesn't Mean High Risk — If You Play It Right

Reframes high-deductible health plans paired with an HSA as a strategy, not a downgrade, when members understand how to use the account.

Video 31. The Quiet Leak: Dependent Eligibility Audits

Explains how covering ineligible dependents (an ex-spouse, an adult child who's aged out) quietly drains plan dollars for everyone.

Video 32. What a Smart Small-Business Plan Design Actually Looks Like

Pulls the employer-side levers together — level funding, local/community networks, DPC access, a slimmer Rx network — as a capstone for business-owner viewers.

Video 33. You Are the Last Line of Defense

Series capstone. Recaps the arc from "you're buying something" through the oversight gap to the shopping playbook, and closes with a direct call to action.
